Does the classifier stay a proxy for retrieval when the simulator is wrong?

80 planets retrieved under both conditions, scored by the frozen
classifier on the identical arrays. Retrieval uses the generating forward model,
so every disagreement below is a lower bound.

condition   retrieval  classifier  they agree  both right agreed, wrong
clean           95.0%       95.0%       92.5%       91.2%          1.4%
haze            55.0%       61.3%       91.2%       53.8%         41.1%

Retrieval loses 40.0 points, the classifier 33.7.
Agreement between them falls from 92.5% to 91.2%.

Agreement is not a validity check. The two methods agree almost as often under the
haze as when clean, but the share of those agreements that are wrong rises from
1.4% to 41.1%: they fail together, on the same planets.

The accuracy difference between the two methods under the haze is not significant
(McNemar exact p = 0.125, 1 planet vs 6), so the claim is that retrieval buys no
robustness to an ingredient it does not model, not that the classifier beats it.
