Sets: (a) committed 64 + 24 (+17); (b) the 65-label rule-consistent panel set of label_sensitivity.py plus the later batches;
(c) its 59-label judgement-free set plus the later batches; (d) one uniform rule across all three batches: a label is scored only if
every printed free-chemistry variant (label_sources.csv notes for the panel, fourth_batch_second_reader.csv for the fourth batch,
independent_data_survey.md for the 17 further labels) lies on one side of 10^-4; variants are pooled per planet across reductions.
A 1-sigma interval is one variant; a quoted limit (any level) is a variant where it is the reported constraint; a second level
printed for the same posterior is not a separate variant. (d') the same, with limits that lie above 10^-4 treated as uninformative
rather than crossing. The ensemble has stored verdicts only on the committed labels, so paired differences cover labels both designs
have; labels that enter under a rule are scored for the baselines only. Writes results/round3/real_label_rules.txt.

== notes
  The ensemble (results/round3/real_C+B-mlp.csv) carries verdicts only on the committed labels, so a label that enters under a rule is scored for the baselines alone and is left out of every paired difference; the baselines' full-set scores are printed beside.
  Variants are pooled per planet across reductions and papers, as the fourth batch did ("any printed free-chemistry variant on the planet's JWST data"); the panel's committed-unscored cells not listed above stay unscored under both readings (straddles or limit above -4 or molecule not reported or literature disagrees across -4).
  The 17 further labels: the variant lists come from independent_data_survey.md (Tables A1 and B3), not from results/independent_test/, whose SOURCES.md repeats only the V1298 Tau c and HAT-P-14 b values; the survey warns that seven single-planet HST papers were not read, so those cells are kept on the population-study values alone.
  Bootstrap: 20,000 resamples of planet units (reductions of one planet kept together), seed 407, sum of per-unit paired differences, as in checks/round3_real_new.py; unlike that script, the HST and JWST reductions of V1298 Tau c form one unit here (47 units over all, not 48), which moves no interval by more than 0.1.
  (d') relaxes only the leaving of committed labels; it lets no further cell enter (otherwise the withdrawn K2-18 b H2O labels of the Madhusudhan and Schmidt reductions would re-enter on the Hu reduction's 1-sigma limit alone). The Hu reduction's own H2O label leaves under (d) because the AURA limits in the same paper, -3.52 and -3.57 at 2 sigma, are its reported constraints and lie above -4; it stays under (d').
