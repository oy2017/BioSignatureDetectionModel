The held-out grids through the full JWST-to-Ariel operator against the shortcut (mask + independent per-bin noise): 50 source models per reduction and grid (seeded subset), 20 noise draws per model and precision.
Balanced credit = mean of the pooled credit on truly present (v > -4) and truly absent labels; right 1, split or withheld 0.5, wrong 0; draws averaged per label. 95 % planet-bootstrap percentile intervals (2000 replicates; planets = reductions with -alt/-hu/-hm stripped, resampled with replacement; differences paired within replicate).
Full operator: native source model averaged over each JWST point's interval (jwst_to_ariel.sample_clean), Gaussian noise per point at its error, align_offsets with the pipeline's reference mode (most points; WASP-39 b: SOSS order 1 with MIRI levelled on the benchmark model), combine (overlap fraction x inverse variance), bins covered < 0.5 flat-filled at the measured mean; Tier-1: the payload sigma per bin added to the converted spectrum. The ensemble is refitted with round3_learner's seeds (checked below); routing and the re-implemented classifier use panel_tier1.score as goyal_test.run_frozen does.
