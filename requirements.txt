# Versions below are pinned to those reported in the manuscript. This matters:
# results are sensitive to them. XGBoost in particular returns measurably
# different accuracies across major releases (3.3.x vs the pinned 3.2.0), and
# because the model uses subsample=0.8, accuracy also shifts by roughly 0.4
# percentage points with training-row ordering even at a fixed random_state.
# XGBoost metrics are therefore best reported as a mean over several restarts
# rather than from a single run.
