Confined-haze control: vertical structure vs wavelength dependence

The deck-versus-haze divergence at comparable suppression is attributed to the
wavelength dependence of the aerosol opacity. The two prescriptions also differ
in vertical structure - the deck truncates at a pressure level, the haze fills
the column - so this control holds the haze density fixed and confines it to
P < 1e3 Pa, the matched deck's own altitude.

All arms are rendered on the committed test planets (paired), so accuracies are
directly comparable. Frozen pipeline, XGBoost.

Confining the haze changes accuracy by 2.0 points and leaves the band-resolved
signature essentially unchanged; part of that difference is the slightly lower
overall suppression (0.46 against 0.44). Vertical placement is therefore not
what separates the haze from the deck, and the attribution to wavelength
dependence stands.
