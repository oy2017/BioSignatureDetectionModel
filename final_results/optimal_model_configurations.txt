Optimal Model Configurations
============================
Date: January 6, 2026
Dataset: H2 Atmosphere Biosignatures

1. XGBoost (Aggressive) - **Best Performer (~86.6%)**
-----------------------------------------------------
*   n_estimators: 300
*   max_depth: 5
*   learning_rate: 0.2
*   subsample: 1.0
*   eval_metric: 'logloss'
*   Features: PCA Components 2-102 (Scaled)

2. MLP (Deep & Wide) - **Robust Performer (~85.0%)**
----------------------------------------------------
*   Architecture: [512, 256, 128] -> Output(1)
*   Hidden Activation: ReLU + BatchNormalization
*   Dropout Rate: 0.4
*   Optimizer: Adam (learning_rate=0.0005)
*   Batch Size: 128
*   Epochs: ~60 (with Early Stopping, Patience=10)
*   Features: PCA Components 0-100 (Scaled)

3. Random Forest (Tuned) - **Baseline (~83.6%)**
------------------------------------------------
*   n_estimators: 300
*   max_depth: None
*   min_samples_leaf: 1
*   min_samples_split: 2
*   class_weight: 'balanced'
*   Features: PCA Components 2-102 (Scaled)

4. CNN (ResNet) - **Underperformer on Small Data (~50%)**
---------------------------------------------------------
*   Architecture: Custom 1D ResNet (3 Residual Blocks: 32, 64, 128 filters)
*   Kernel Size: 7
*   Pooling: MaxPool + GlobalAverage
*   Optimizer: Adam (learning_rate=0.0001)
*   Features: Raw Spectra (Scaled)
*   Note: Requires significantly more data or augmentation to converge effectively.
