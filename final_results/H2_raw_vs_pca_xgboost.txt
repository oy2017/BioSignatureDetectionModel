Does the PCA rotation cost XGBoost accuracy? (eval_raw_vs_pca_xgboost.py)
Identical tuned hyperparameters, identical five test sets.

  config              5-set mean          F1        pooled
  raw               62.74% +/- 0.91   F1  63.17%   pooled  62.74%  (n=550)
  raw + z-score     62.96% +/- 0.93   F1  63.46%   pooled  62.96%  (n=550)
  PCA-102           87.92% +/- 2.29   F1  87.94%   pooled  87.91%  (n=102)

The PCA rotation is load-bearing for the tree model: raw-basis XGBoost
trails the published pipeline by ~25 points, and per-channel z-scoring
does not close the gap (histogram splits are scale-invariant), so the
gain comes from the rotation, not the rescaling.
